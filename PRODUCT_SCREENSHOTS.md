# Product screenshots

Add approved product screenshots to `public/product-screenshots/` using these filenames:

- `distribution-1.png`, `distribution-2.png`, `distribution-3.png`
- `underwriting-1.png`, `underwriting-2.png`, `underwriting-3.png`
- `claims-1.png`, `claims-2.png`, `claims-3.png`
